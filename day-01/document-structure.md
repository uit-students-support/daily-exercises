Based on your research and understanding, answer the following question:

**Q1.**
A graduation thesis is typically organized into three main parts: front matter, main matter and back matter. What pages or sections are typically included in each part? List them in the order in which they appear in the thesis.

**A1.**
Three main parts include:
- Front matter:
    + Title page
    + Abstract
    + Acknowledgments
    + Table of Contents
    + List of Figures
    + List of Tables
    + List of Abbreviations / Symbols
    + List of Listings

- Main matter:
    + Introduction
    + Related Work
    + Methodology
    + Results
    + Discussion
    + Conclusion

- Back matter:
    + References
    + Appendices

**Q2.**
What other elements are included in the thesis besides the main text?

**A2.**
The thesis also includes page numbers, headers, and footers.
